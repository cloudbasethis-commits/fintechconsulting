In Côte d'Ivoire, mobile money has become an essential payment method. For a business — retail, services, financial institution — accepting Orange Money, MTN MoMo, Moov Money and Wave is no longer a convenience, it is a commercial necessity.

## Why accept mobile money

- **Reach more customers**: a large part of the population uses mobile money daily, sometimes without a bank account.
- **Get paid faster**: payments are instant and confirmed in real time.
- **Reduce cash**: less cash means fewer security risks and simpler accounting.
- **Improve customer experience**: a smooth payment journey increases conversion.

## The different ways to integrate mobile money

### Simple merchant payment
A merchant code or QR code lets you receive payments without heavy technical integration. Ideal to start, but limited in automation and reconciliation.

### API integration
For businesses that want to **automate** collection, reconciliation and tracking, integration via **operator or aggregator APIs** is the right approach. It connects mobile money directly to your system (POS, ERP, e-commerce platform, Core Banking).

### Multi-operator aggregation
Rather than integrating each operator separately, a **payment aggregator** offers a single entry point to all channels (Orange, MTN, Moov, Wave, cards). This is often the most efficient solution for a multi-channel business.

## Points of attention

### Reconciliation
Receiving a payment is not enough: it must be **automatically matched** to the corresponding order or invoice. A good integration automates this reconciliation and avoids manual errors.

### Security
Payment flows must be secured end to end, with detection of anomalies and suspicious transactions.

### Fees and margin
Each channel has its pricing. A good integration strategy optimizes costs by intelligently routing flows.

### Compliance
Financial flows must respect the BCEAO framework and data protection (ARTCI).

## The right starting point

Before integrating, ask yourself three questions: which channels do my customers actually use? What level of automation am I aiming for? How will I reconcile and secure the flows?

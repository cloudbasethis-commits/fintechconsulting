Opening a bank branch in a secondary town, equipping a mining or agricultural site, connecting a distant warehouse: in Côte d'Ivoire, **connectivity** remains a challenge as soon as you leave the major urban centers. Yet reliable solutions exist. This guide presents **VSAT, fiber optic and radio links** to durably connect all your sites.

## The problem: uneven coverage

Fiber optic and 4G cover Abidjan and the main cities well, but leave entire areas poorly served. However, a branch or industrial site needs a **reliable and permanent** connection to operate: banking transactions, monitoring, communications, security, data feeds. An outage means a halted operation and unhappy customers.

## Connectivity solutions

### VSAT (satellite)
**VSAT** (Very Small Aperture Terminal) provides broadband connectivity **via satellite**, independent of terrestrial infrastructure. It is the ideal solution for areas not covered by fiber or 4G: mining sites, agricultural sites, rural branches. Its major advantage: it works **everywhere**, including the most remote areas of Côte d'Ivoire. Discover our [telecoms & connectivity](/en/services/telecoms) expertise.

### Fiber optic
Where available, fiber offers the best throughput and latency. It is the reference solution for urban sites, headquarters and data centers.

### Radio links (microwave)
Radio links interconnect two relatively close sites without trenching, with good throughput. Useful to link buildings, connect an annex or bypass an obstacle.

## Technology comparison

| Technology | Coverage | Throughput | Ideal for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fiber optic | Urban areas | Very high | HQ, urban sites |
| VSAT (satellite) | Everywhere | High | Remote sites, rural areas |
| Radio link | Nearby sites | High | Local interconnection |
| 4G/LTE | Covered areas | Variable | Backup, mobility |

## Interconnecting several sites securely

A multi-site business needs more than a simple Internet connection: it must **interconnect its sites securely**. Private networks (VPN, MPLS) link branches, headquarters and industrial sites into a unified network, as if on the same local network. This interconnection must rely on a [solid IT infrastructure](/en/services/infrastructure-cloud) and mastered [security](/en/services/cybersecurite).

## Staying in control: monitoring

A link that goes down unnoticed means a halted operation. Proactive **link monitoring** lets you:

- detect incidents before they impact operations;
- measure the quality of service actually delivered;
- anticipate capacity needs and plan upgrades.

## Choosing the right combination

In practice, the best architecture often combines several technologies: **fiber** where it exists, **VSAT** for isolated sites, **radio links** as a complement, with **4G as backup**, all monitored and securely interconnected.
